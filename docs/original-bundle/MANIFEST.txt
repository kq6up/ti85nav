TI-85 CELESTIAL NAVIGATION PACKAGE
==================================
Assembled 29 September 2026. All files current as of this date.

PROGRAMS -- convert with ASCII85P.EXE, see TRANSFER.txt
-------------------------------------------------------
    SIGHT.ASC     V8    e9df12029451ca23c5f8951534dba464
        Full walk-through. Follows the reduction sheet top to bottom and
        finishes with Hc, Z, Zn and the intercept. Refraction uses the
        OpenCPN Celestial Navigation plugin model, so SIGHT and the plugin
        agree exactly when you cross-check.

    SIGHT.85P     V8    2cc7a70850f352f1bcc09d4d469ea668
        Pre-converted binary. Verified line by line against SIGHT.ASC --
        348 lines, zero mismatches, checksum validates.

    SETUP.ASC           9cd5fa19e66e0a9f962057c8c26017a7
        DR position, index correction, height of eye, temperature,
        pressure. Run once before SIGHT or SIGHT throws ERROR 14.

    SEA.ASC             5ce1bc530415890e89ab4b99a475f24e
        Hs to Ho on its own, sea horizon. Computes dip and refraction
        (Bennett) from height of eye, temperature and pressure.

    AIR.ASC             4a2d88971f5189137dbd2633e2e32896
        Hs to Ho on its own, Air Almanac style -- dip, refraction and moon
        parallax come from the tables.

    HO229.ASC     V1.3  c9137cb6a51e0f2ee91cb7d7f80a59c0
        Sight reduction only: AP latitude, LHA and declination in, Hc in
        DD.MMm and Z and Zn in degrees out. Drop-in replacement for the
        Pub. 229 table lookup when you are working the paper form.

CHANGE IN THIS PACKAGE
----------------------
HO229 V1.3 only. The declination prompt now reads

    TOTAL DEC N+ S-
    d-CORR APPLIED

rather than a bare "DEC", so it is unambiguous that the program wants the
total declination from item 10 of the form -- tabulated value plus the
d-correction -- and not the raw hourly figure off the daily page. The
arithmetic is untouched; all five self-test values in the primer are
unchanged.
